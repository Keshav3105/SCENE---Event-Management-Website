InnovateX 2026: Campus Hackathon

About the Event

InnovateX is our university’s premier annual 36-hour hackathon bringing together students across engineering, design, and business disciplines. Participants will form multidisciplinary teams of 2 to 4 members to conceptualize, prototype, and pitch tech-driven solutions for challenges across Smart Campus Sustainability, HealthTech, and Generative AI applications. Mentors from leading tech firms will provide hands-on guidance throughout the weekend.

Event Highlights & Schedule

  • Day 1 (09:00 AM): Opening Keynote, Track Announcements & Team Formation.
  • Day 1 (02:00 PM): Hands-on API & Cloud Sandbox Mentorship Workshop.
  • Day 2 (10:00 AM): Mid-way Code Review and Pitch Coaching Sessions.
  • Day 2 (04:00 PM): Final Project Expo, Jury Demos, and Awards Ceremony.

Eligibility & Requirements

Open to all enrolled undergraduate and postgraduate students. Bring your university ID card, your laptop, charger, and an enthusiastic mindset! Meals, snacks, and internet connectivity are provided throughout the duration of the hackathon.
